Dynamite HeaddyRandomizer

Shuffle Headcases, recolour the stages and bring back the Japanese balance. Everything runs in your browser: your ROM is never uploaded.

1Your ROM

Dynamite Headdy (USA, Europe), as a .md, .bin or .gen file. No ROM is provided here.

2Options

Start from a preset or set things yourself. Same seed + same options = same ROM, so you can race a seed with friends.
